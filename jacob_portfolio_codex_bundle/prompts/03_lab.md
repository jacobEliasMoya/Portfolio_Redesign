# Prompt — Lab

Implement a `LAB` chapter after What I Build and before the coral contact footer.

Purpose:

Lab is the less-polished experimental counterpart to Selected Work. It can contain AI/agent experiments, Go/backend work, small utilities, technical prototypes, design experiments, notes, and unfinished ideas.

Visual rules:

- `LAB`, never `LAB.`.
- Place Lab inside a pastel container/field that clearly belongs to the established visual system.
- Keep it playful and exploratory without becoming a bento dashboard.
- Use the same condensed/mono type hierarchy as the homepage.
- A small sculptural visual is acceptable, but 3D is not required on every Lab item.

Information hierarchy:

- Lab title
- very short explanation of what Lab is
- a small set of experiment entries
- status/technology metadata where useful
- clear navigation to the full Lab index or detail entries

Lab should feel like evidence of curiosity and active exploration, not an attempt to inflate every experiment into a case study.
