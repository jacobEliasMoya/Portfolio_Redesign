# Prompt — What I Build Interaction

Implement the desktop What I Build interaction from the approved brief.

Layout:

- Left: compact sticky title block reading `WHAT I BUILD`, max two lines.
- Right: vertical series of thick capability pills.
- The left title region must not visually extend to the same height as the full right-side sequence.

Capabilities:

- Creative Development
- Full-Stack Websites
- Digital Experiences
- AI-Driven Development
- Technical Direction

Behavior:

- Native page scrolling only; no scroll-jacking.
- As a capability enters the active zone, its thick pill expands vertically into the active content surface.
- Reveal concise supporting copy and contextual technology/tool labels.
- The pill itself becomes the panel; do not nest a generic card inside it.
- The next capability should approach naturally as the active item releases.
- When the section completes, sticky behavior releases and the page continues normally into Lab.

Interaction should remain understandable without animation and respect reduced-motion preferences.

Avoid timeline lines, circles, progress rails, tiny chips, or conventional accordion styling.
