# Design Decisions

## Locked / strongly preferred

- Hero direction is strong; refine rather than redesign.
- Selected Work direction is strong.
- A minimal Experience section belongs on the homepage after Selected Work. It should show four distinct career steps—VAREP, BizIQ Web Developer I, BizIQ Web Developer II, and Innervi—without becoming a full résumé, and should link to the complete résumé.
- What I Build is one of the defining homepage interactions.
- Footer/contact direction is strong.
- Lab is approved as a concept.
- Lab should sit inside a pastel container/field.
- Spell it `LAB`, not `LAB.`.
- Add meaningful vertical whitespace between major homepage sections.
- `AI-Driven Development` belongs in What I Build as a capability.
- What I Build title should be one or two lines, not `WHAT / I / BUILD` on three lines.
- Sticky title region must be shorter than the scrolling capability content beside it.
- Capability pills should be thick/substantial.
- Technology appears inside expanded capabilities rather than as a generic separate icon grid.
- Desktop may use sticky-scroll behavior; mobile should adapt rather than imitate it literally.
- Selected Experience uses the same substantial horizontal pill/accordion grammar as What I Build, with four rows preserving all four career steps.
- Selected Experience sits on a cool powder-blue/lavender field that clearly separates it from the warm off-white page canvas.

## Rejected

- Three-line `WHAT / I / BUILD` title.
- Making sticky title and capability parent the same height.
- Decorative line-and-circle/timeline treatments.
- Putting the title inside a generic light-gray background card solely for containment.
- Standard logo/skill badge tech section.
- Generic `Part Designer / Part Engineer` card treatment for the homepage.
- Conventional three-card process sections.
- Cookie-cutter editorial/process diagrams that visually break from the pastel 3D theme.
- Adding sections merely because portfolios traditionally include them. Experience is an intentional exception because it provides necessary professional context.
- A 3D carousel, Rolodex, prism, or oversized project-card treatment for Experience or What I Build on the homepage.

## Explorations that may still be useful elsewhere

The design/engineering duality and large `I MAKE THINGS` concepts had strong hero energy. They were not considered good mid-page sections, but their conceptual language may be useful on an About page, Lab landing page, case-study opening, or future hero experiment.

The four-faced pastel prism and front-facing carousel/Rolodex were useful motion explorations, but neither belongs on the current homepage. Their oversized cards competed with Selected Work and repeated too much interaction machinery. Keep the exploration images as process documentation; do not treat them as implementation references.

The next Experience/What I Build concept to revisit is one coordinated scroll chapter: Experience initially occupies the stage, then moves aside as What I Build enters the space it vacates. The exact layout, clipping, timing, and whether the stage should be pinned remain unresolved. Exploration `15_shared_sticky_stage_midpoint.png` did not capture the intended result and is not an implementation reference.

The homepage LAB remains the approved compact pastel strip with the arch, sphere, terrazzo cube, orange rings, and blue stairs. LAB may lead to a deeper blog/editorial route later, but do not turn the homepage strip itself into an article feed.

## Why this matters

The portfolio became stronger as sections were removed. Do not equate completeness with section count. The homepage should be concise and memorable; deeper evidence belongs inside project pages, Lab entries, About, résumé, or supporting routes.
