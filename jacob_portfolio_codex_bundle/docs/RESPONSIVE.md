# Responsive Direction

## Principle

Mobile is not a compressed desktop screenshot. Preserve identity, hierarchy, and content while changing interaction models when the desktop behavior no longer makes sense.

## Mobile priorities

- Keep the bold condensed hero typography.
- Preserve hero artwork, but allow deliberate cropping/recomposition.
- Maintain substantial section spacing.
- Keep project cards highly visual.
- Convert What I Build from sticky-scroll into direct touch expansion.
- Keep Lab clearly separated as its own pastel field/container.
- Maintain the coral footer/contact ending.

## Navigation

Use a simple accessible mobile menu. Do not create an elaborate full-screen interaction unless it materially improves the site.

## Hero

- Headline remains dominant.
- Avoid shrinking it until it loses personality.
- Artwork may move below/behind/alongside the type depending on available width.
- CTA remains obvious and thumb-accessible.

## Selected Work

Prefer horizontal swipe/scroll or a clear vertical stack over squeezing multiple desktop cards into one viewport.

If horizontal scrolling is used:

- make the next item partially visible as an affordance
- preserve normal links and keyboard behavior where applicable
- do not trap scroll

## What I Build

Desktop concept: sticky title + scrolling expanding pills.

Mobile concept: heading + thick touch-friendly panels that expand on tap.

Do not recreate desktop sticky behavior if it causes long empty areas or confusing scroll states.

## Lab

Keep Lab visually playful but readable. A compact card/index system is acceptable here if it feels intentionally different from Selected Work and not like a generic bento grid.

## Footer

Allow the footer to stack vertically. Keep the large CTA and strong coral field, but prioritize contact usability over preserving exact desktop geometry.
