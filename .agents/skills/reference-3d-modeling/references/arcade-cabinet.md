# Arcade cabinet: monitor-parallel playing surface

## User-approved direction

The portfolio can be experienced as a game inside a buildable 3D arcade cabinet. The user accepted a simple graphite/silver cabinet, pixel marquee, cyan accent and a separate physical joystick/button deck. The initial cinematic border was rejected as insufficiently practical. A traditional backward-tilted recessed screen was then corrected: **the gameplay screen must be parallel to the user's actual monitor in the playing view**.

- Playing camera: centered straight-on, screen normal aligned to camera forward axis, screen edges horizontal and vertical. No keystone, CRT curvature or backward rake. The screen can be vertical in model space, with the playing camera at its center height and zero pitch/yaw.
- Display surface: flat and visually flush with a thin bezel. Cabinet depth belongs in side panels, bevels, marquee and the projecting deck. Avoid a tunnel around the viewport.
- Forms: extruded side profiles, body panels, shallow marquee box, bezel ring, display plane, angled control slab, short joystick shaft/faceted ball and cylindrical buttons. Separate parts where they need independent material, pivot or interaction.
- Style: matte graphite, silver edges, restrained cyan, existing Sabo Filled lettering from the project when modeled. Generated lettering is a concept approximation, not font source artwork.
- Gameplay: preserve Earth and Jakes Space as the central focus and leave ample usable screen area. A 16:9 opening is a proposed construction proportion for the reference pack, not a user-approved fixed responsive size.
